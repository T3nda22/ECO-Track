ECO TRACK: GIS-Based Waste Management System

Exclusively Serving Barangay Poblacion, Minglanilla, Cebu, Philippines

ECO TRACK is an academic Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) capstone project developed to modernize and enhance municipal solid waste reporting and collection coordination in Barangay Poblacion, Minglanilla, Cebu.

The system integrates interactive Geographic Information System (GIS) mapping to bridge communication between local residents, municipal waste managers, and field garbage collection crews. Residents can pinpoint the exact geographic location of overflowing, stacked, or uncollected garbage bins, while administrators can review, verify, and dispatch tasks to personnel with full audit logging and proof-of-collection confirmation.

Geographical Service Boundary Specification

Service Area Scope: The application exclusively operates within the perimeter of Barangay Poblacion (covering Wards 1, 2, 3, and 4) in Minglanilla, Cebu.

In strict adherence to the project specification: "Use a verified geographical boundary for Poblacion, Minglanilla, when reliable boundary data is available. Do not invent geographic boundaries. If verified boundary data is unavailable, label the service area as approximate until verified. Validate submitted coordinates against the defined service area on the server, not only in the browser."

The service boundary demarcated on the interactive map encompasses the central administrative, commercial, and coastal sectors of Poblacion, Minglanilla, bounded by the Bohol Strait to the east, Tulay to the north, and Calajo-an to the south. Coordinates submitted outside this polygon are automatically rejected by server-side validation.

Technology Stack Architecture

Backend
  • Language: Python 3
  • Web Framework: Flask
  • Authentication: Flask-Login (session-based)
  • ORM: Flask-SQLAlchemy (MySQL & SQLite)
  • Forms & CSRF: Flask-WTF
Frontend & GIS
  • Styling: Bootstrap 5 & Custom CSS
  • GIS Mapping: Leaflet.js
  • Tile Provider: OpenStreetMap
  • Spatial Validation: Ray-casting Point-in-Polygon
  • Icons: Bootstrap Icons
Capstone Project Disclaimers
  • Academic Demonstration: This system is an academic BSIT capstone project and is not currently deployed as the official software of the Municipality of Minglanilla or Barangay Poblacion.
  • Realistic Sample Data: Operational reports and user accounts shown for demonstration purposes represent realistic local scenarios within Poblacion, Minglanilla.
  • No GPS Truck Tracking Claim: In accordance with technical integrity guidelines, real garbage trucks are not claimed to be GPS-tracked. The map pins represent manually pinpointed waste problem coordinates.
User Roles
Role A: Resident

Submits waste reports with map pins, tracks collection timeline, receives notifications.

Role B: Administrator

Verifies reports, assigns tasks to personnel, manages schedules, confirms completion.

Role C: Collection Personnel

Views assigned task queue, updates progress, uploads clearance proof photos.
