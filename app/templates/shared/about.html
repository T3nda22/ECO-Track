A Proposed Waste Management System for Minglanilla, Cebu

Community Reporting System with Geographic Information System (GIS) Visualization • Piloted in Minglanilla Poblacion

In Minglanilla, Cebu, effective waste management requires timely information about the location and condition of waste bins and areas that require waste collection. This system computerizes and improves the process of reporting, locating, monitoring, and managing waste collection concerns using Geographic Information System (GIS) technology.

Review of Related Literature & Synthesis

The proposed system draws on three proven strands from local and foreign studies while removing dedicated hardware dependencies (such as RFID readers, GSM modules, and IoT sensors) that a barangay may not be able to procure, install, and sustain:

Adriatico & Javellana (2017) — Local
Demonstrated that geotagging pickup points and tracking garbage bin status on a web and mobile platform is feasible and acceptable in a Philippine barangay setting.
Alvarico, Arricivita, & Cruz (2024) — Local
Showed how web map visualization and location-based notifications help residents and collection personnel coordinate around waste collection.
Arebey et al. (2009) — Foreign
Integrated GIS to monitor solid waste collection operations from end to end in real time.
Draz et al. (2017) — Foreign
Established that monitoring bin status and GPS coordinates and alerting authorities when bins are full enables timely collection action.
Research Gap Addressed
Existing prototypes rely on dedicated IoT/RFID hardware and do not collect observations directly from residents. This software-only platform turns community observation into location-tagged, map-based waste reports: residents pinpoint full bins on a GIS map, administrators and garbage collection personnel view these reports as markers on the same map, and personnel mark reported bins as Cleaned or Collected so administrators can monitor which concerns remain Outstanding.

Scope and Limitations

Project Scope

  • Resident Map Reporting: Reporting of waste concerns by residents, who pinpoint the location of full garbage bins on a map.
  • Recording of Waste Information: Recording of report location, photo, time submitted, and status.
  • Shared GIS Map Visualization: Viewing of reported bins as markers on a GIS-based map by administrators and garbage collection personnel.
  • Personnel Status Updates: Updating of report status by garbage collection personnel to mark bins as Cleaned or Collected.
  • Administrator Monitoring: Monitoring of concerns by administrators, covering which reports remain Outstanding and which have been Addressed.
  • Geographical Coverage: Limited to the Municipality of Minglanilla, Cebu (piloted in Minglanilla Poblacion).

Project Limitations

  • Does not physically collect, transport, segregate, process, recycle, or dispose of garbage.
  • Does not manage garbage trucks, collection equipment, or other physical waste management resources.
  • Does not integrate with sensor or IoT hardware for automatic bin fill-level detection; fullness is reported manually by users.
  • Cannot independently verify the accuracy of user-submitted reports.
  • Requires an internet connection to use.
  • Does not cover areas outside the Municipality of Minglanilla, Cebu.

Intended Users & Beneficiaries

1. Residents / Community Members

Pinpoint on a map the location of garbage bins that are full and in need of cleaning or collection, and submit reports with photos.

2. Waste Management Personnel

View submitted reports as markers on the GIS-based map to identify areas requiring attention, and mark reported bins as Cleaned or Collected.

3. Local Government / Administrators

View reported bins on the same GIS-based map and monitor which waste-related concerns have been Addressed and which remain Outstanding.
